The Wandering Mood Djinn

Que

Before playlists, before algorithms, before the endless scroll, there was rhythm. Not music. Not yet. Something older. A pulse that moved through everything: wind and fire, oceans and stars, people and the quiet spaces between them. Most never noticed it. Some felt it. Only Que could shape it. He was not born. He formed from the space between moments, a flicker in the quiet, a shift in energy, a feeling you could not name but knew was real. Que wanders through states of being. He listens for the mood beneath the mood, then nudges the atmosphere toward balance. If the right song ever found you at the perfect moment, you may have already crossed paths with him.
