## Que — The Mood Djinn

No one knows where Que came from.

Some say he’s been here longer than the cities themselves—moving through streets, rooftops, cafés, and quiet places where people pause without realizing why.

Que doesn’t speak. He listens.

To the rhythm of everything.

The way a crowded room hums.
The shift in energy before the rain.
The silence that follows a long day.

He moves between these moments—what he calls *territories*.
States of being that people pass through without noticing.

Focus.
Drift.
Tension.
Release.

Most people feel them.
Que understands them.

And sometimes… he nudges them.

A song at the right time.
A change in atmosphere.
A moment that feels like it was meant for you.

That’s not random.

That’s Que.

He’s not here to control the mood.

He’s here to guide it.

To help people find balance in a world that’s always shifting.

You don’t find Que.

You notice him.

And once you do…
you start to feel the rhythm too.
