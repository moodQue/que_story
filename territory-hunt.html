Powered by the Mood Intelligence System

Territory Hunt isn't random. The MIS reads lunar phases, social sentiment trends, YouTube creator signals, and a live community engagement index — then plots Que's position across a VAC coordinate space (Valence · Arousal · Control). The territories Que visits are a direct reflection of what the collective mood of the internet actually looks like right now.

Waning moon? Expect more time in the Hollow Grounds. High arousal weeks push toward the Storm Lands or the Lit Realms. The Wandering appears when the signal is unclear — when the mood of the world refuses to be named.
